IMPORTANT BEHAVIOR
- xAI API key is never committed or embedded in source. User enters it in Settings.
- xAI is paid. Do not add paid speculative prefetch without explicit design approval.
- xAI full export is chunked and streamed to the output file; do not accumulate an entire book's audio in RAM.
- REST is used rather than WebSocket because the app already has a reliable file/chunk playback pipeline and direct WebSocket client keys should not be exposed.
- Google Gemini remains available but is not required for xAI operation.
